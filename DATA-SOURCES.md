# City database

City data is derived from GeoNames cities15000, countryInfo and admin1CodesASCII, retrieved 2026-11-02 from https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/.

GeoNames: https://www.geonames.org/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

This database includes populated places with population over 15,000 and capitals, per GeoNames. It is broad worldwide coverage, not every village or town. Coordinates and alternate names are supplied by GeoNames. Labels are combined with first-level administrative region and country. Selected English names from the app are retained. Duplicate city/region labels are consolidated using the highest population entry.
